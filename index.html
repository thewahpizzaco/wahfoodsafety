🛡️ Written HACCP Process Flow & SFBB Safe Methods (The Wah Pizza Co.)

Cardiff Council EHO Reference

1. Purchase & Delivery

Suppliers: A Di Maria, Tesco, Lidl.
Control: Chilled goods (Mozzarella) checked on arrival (target ≤ 4–5°C), packaging intact, allergen labels verified, receipts kept for traceability.

2. Chilled & Ambient Storage

CCP: Domestic fridge maintained at ≤ 4°C (probed daily).
Control: Commercial stock segregated from household items. Open ingredients decanted into food-grade tubs with 3-day Use-By labels (Day + 2).

3. 24h Room-Temp Dough

Control: Flour, water, salt & yeast only (no dairy/egg/meat; low $a_w$ & competitive yeast fermentation). Ambient room temp logged daily (~20°C) in covered food-grade proofing boxes, then baked >400°C.

4. Pre-Cooking & ≤90m Cooling

CCP: Cooked toppings (mushrooms for White Base pizza) shallow-tray cooled to ambient within ≤ 90 minutes and transferred immediately to ≤ 4°C fridge.

5. Baking & Reheating

CCP: Gozney Arc LPG ovens run at > 400°C (IR verified). Pre-cooked toppings reheated to ≥ 75°C (visual check: cheese melted & toppings bubbling hot throughout during 60–90s bake; core probe validation during batch prep).
Acrylamide Control: Active turning with turning peel; heavily charred bases discarded.

6. Boxed Pickup Service

Control: Direct collection on time in new food-grade pizza boxes (no hot-holding). Dairy-free Marinara orders clearly box-labelled. LPG leak-checked with soapy water before every session.

7. Food Contact Materials & Packaging

Control: Certified food-grade pizza boxes & greaseproof liner sheets stored dry off the floor in sealed clean containers. 1-step-forward order records retained for customer recall traceability.

8. Waste, FOG & Water Potability

Control: Welsh Water mains potable supply. Commercial food scraps and cheese residues scraped into separate Wales Workplace Commercial Recycling food caddy (zero fats, oils, or food solids down domestic drains).
SFBB Safe Methods & HACCP Pillar Summary:
• Cross-Contamination: Strictly vegetarian pizza menu (zero raw meat/poultry/fish on premises); dedicated 2nd handwash sink with Jantex antibacterial soap; clear coeliac disclaimer regarding airborne wheat flour.
• Cleaning: Two-stage cleaning using disposable blue roll and Jantex Multi-Surface Sanitiser (BS EN 1276, adhering strictly to manufacturer dilution and contact time).
• Chilling & Time Limits: ≤ 4°C fridge target, ≤ 90 min rapid cooling for cooked toppings; ambient service toppings monitored under the 4-hour rule.
• Cooking & Baking: > 400°C Gozney Arc bake, ≥ 75°C core reheat for cooked toppings, semolina peel dusting & active peel turning for acrylamide char prevention.
• Management & Waste: 48-hour sickness exclusion rule, monthly digital probe calibration, 5-year LPG hose checks, signed immutable records, and Wales 2024 commercial recycling separation.

📢 Customer Allergen Signposting & Collection Protocol

UK Food Information Regulations & Wales FHRS
1. Before Ordering (Web Storefront & Pickup Point):

All 14 UK regulated allergens are declared directly on The Wah Pizza Co. online ordering page before checkout and on a printed notice at the pickup door.

2. Shared Kitchen Cross-Contact Disclosure:

Customers are informed that all dough contains Wheat Gluten and is stretched/baked in a kitchen and Gozney Arc oven that handles Milk (Mozzarella).

3. Box Labelling on Collection:

Pizzas are cooked fresh to order (Non-Prepacked, not PPDS). Any dairy-free Marinara or allergy-flagged order is prepared with clean tools and clearly marked on the box lid before handover.

Confirmed Kitchen Allergens: Cereals containing Gluten (Wheat Flour) Milk (Mozzarella). Never guess allergens from a product name.
